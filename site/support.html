Aero is a clean, fast player for the M3U playlists, Xtream logins and XMLTV guides you already have, built for a Mac on the big screen.

Getting started

1. Add your playlist
Open Aero and go to Settings ▸ Playlists. Click Add playlist and paste your M3U link, or click Add Xtream account and enter your server, username and password. Aero ships empty. It has no built-in channels.
2. (Optional) Add a guide
Many playlists bring their own guide. If yours doesn't, paste your XMLTV link into the playlist's Guide link field to see what's on now and next.
3. Watch
Pick a channel and play. Aero remembers your favourites and the last channel you watched.

Free and Pro

Every feature is yours to try free for 14 days. After that, one playlist or account stays free for as long as you like. Aero Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It adds more playlists and accounts, plus catch-up, live rewind and archive playback.

Common questions

Where do I get a playlist?
From an IPTV service you subscribe to. Aero does not provide channels or playlists and is not affiliated with any provider.
Where are Movies and Series?
They come from an Xtream account. A plain M3U playlist usually holds live TV only, so the Movies and Series tabs stay empty with one.
A channel won't play.
Most playback issues are with the stream or your provider, not Aero. Check your playlist link is current. Then try the other player: Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Player switches between Native, Aero's own player, and VLC.
Voices and lips don't line up.
This is common when sound plays through a soundbar or an amplifier. Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Sound delay holds the sound back until they match.
Fast motion looks jerky.
A channel at 60 frames a second can't show every frame on a screen that refreshes 50 times a second. With the Native player, turn on Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Smooth 60 fps channels.
What formats are supported?
Aero's own player handles HLS and MPEG-TS streams with H.264 and HEVC video, up to 4K. VLC's engine is built in for everything else, and Aero switches to it on its own when a stream needs it.

Contact

Need help? Email support@aeroplay.tv. To help us find the problem quickly, open Settings ▸ Playback, click Copy diagnostics, and paste it into your email. It contains no playlist link or account details.
