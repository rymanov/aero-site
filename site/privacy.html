Privacy Policy

Aero IPTV · Last updated 14 November 2026

Aero IPTV ("Aero", "the app") is a media player for macOS. This policy explains what the app and this website do and do not handle. The short version: the app collects nothing about you. We handle personal information only if you email us or visit this website, as set out below.

Who we are

Aero is made and run by Mikhail Rymanov, an individual in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach him at privacy@aeroplay.tv.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app needs no Aero account, and has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, and no third-party telemetry. It sends us nothing, and we run no servers that your use of Aero passes through. We never sell personal information, and we share it only with the two service providers named below, Google and Cloudflare.

Data stored on your Mac

To work, Aero keeps the following on your Mac: the playlist links and Xtream logins you add (in your Mac's keychain), cached playlist and guide data for a fast start, your favourites, the channels and titles you watched and where you stopped, your settings, and a scrambled copy of your parental PIN if you set one. The app sends none of it to us.

Removing your data

macOS keeps an app's data when you delete the app. To remove what Aero keeps: remove your playlists in Settings ▸ Playlists, which also removes their logins; if you set a parental PIN, reset it in Settings ▸ Manage groups; then quit and delete Aero, and delete the folder ~/Library/Containers/com.rymanov.Aero.

Network connections

To play, Aero connects only to the playlists, guides and Xtream accounts you add, and to the streams, pictures and guide data they list. Like any website you visit, those services see your connection, including your IP address and what you ask them for, and your Xtream server receives your login when Aero signs in. We are not one of those services and cannot see what you watch.

Diagnostics

Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Copy diagnostics puts a short technical summary on your clipboard. It contains no playlist link or account details, and nothing is sent unless you paste it into a message yourself.

Purchases

Aero Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment, and the app only asks Apple whether you own Aero Pro. We receive no payment card or personal details.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and your message, and anything you add to it, such as diagnostics. Writing to us is up to you; we need your email address to reply. We use what you send only to answer you, which is in the legitimate interest of us both. We keep support emails for 12 months after our last reply (or after you wrote, if we never replied), and delete them within the following month. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time. Our email runs on Google Workspace.

This website

aeroplay.tv sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or embedded content from other companies. It is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect the site from attacks, in our legitimate interest in running a safe website. Your IP address is needed to deliver the pages to you. We receive no visitor logs from Cloudflare; Cloudflare keeps its own short-lived traffic and security records under its own privacy policy.

Outside the UK

Google and Cloudflare may process this information outside the UK. Transfers to the United States rely on the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, under which both companies are certified, and on the standard data protection clauses in their contracts. You can ask us for details.

Content

Aero does not host, provide, or supply any channels, streams, or content. You supply your own playlist from a service you are entitled to use, and you are responsible for ensuring your use complies with the law in your region.

Children

Aero is a general-audience media player and is not directed at children. The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your rights

You can ask us at privacy@aeroplay.tv what we hold about you, and to correct it, delete it, or restrict how we use it. You can also object to our use of it. You can also complain to a data protection authority; in the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@aeroplay.tv
